Slow mornings.

Crafted with intention, poured with care.

We believe coffee is more than a routine. It is a moment of pause in a fast-moving world. Our beans are ethically sourced, gently roasted, and prepared with an obsessive attention to detail.

Meet the roaster.

I'm Maya, and Lumina started in a small garage in 2019. What began as a personal obsession with sourcing single-origin beans has grown into a community of people who believe coffee should be intentional.

Every batch is roasted by hand. I spend hours dialing in the temperature, listening to the crack, watching the color shift. There's no algorithm for this—just decades of craft passed down, and a relentless pursuit of perfection.

When you visit, you're not just getting coffee. You're getting a moment with someone who genuinely cares about your morning.

How we do it.

1

Source

We partner directly with farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Kenya. No middlemen. Just conversations with growers who share our values.

2

Roast

Hand-roasted in small batches. We listen to the bean, adjust the heat, and pull at the exact moment it reaches peak potential.

3

Pour

Whether espresso, pour-over, or cappuccino—each cup is made with intention. We take time. We don't rush.
